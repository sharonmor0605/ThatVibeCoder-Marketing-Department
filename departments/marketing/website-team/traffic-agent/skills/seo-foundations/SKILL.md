# seo-foundations

Not tricks. Just making the page legible to machines that send strangers.

## Steps
1. **Title tag**: what the page is + who it's for, under 60 characters, the plain way someone would search for it. The page's job in one line.
2. **Description**: one honest sentence on what's here and why click: it's the ad that shows under the title in results.
3. **One main heading** that matches what the title promised. Section headings say what each section actually is. Machines and skimmers read the same way.
4. **Name the images** in plain words (what the image shows), so they're findable and the page still makes sense if they fail.
5. **One page, one topic.** A page about two things ranks for neither. Split it instead.

## Rules
- Write for the person, tune for the machine, in that order. A page shaped for robots reads wrong to humans and loses both.
- Never stuff words in unnaturally. If it reads like SEO, it's bad SEO.
