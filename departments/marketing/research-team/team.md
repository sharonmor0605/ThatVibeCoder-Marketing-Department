# Research Team

What this team owns: knowing what's happening outside your business. What competitors ship, what's working in your space, what belongs in your marketing brain.

Day 13 puts this team to work feeding your knowledge folder. Each member is a folder here.

## Summon: "run my marketing checkup"
The one command that runs the whole analysis. Say it any time after Day 13 (monthly is the rhythm), and claude runs this order (each step a skill that already exists), results compiled into ONE report:
1. **Researcher** runs competitor-check (what changed out there, tier by tier) and find-outliers (what's overperforming in the space).
2. **Traffic Agent** (marketing/website-team) runs weekly-traffic-read: what came, from where, one action.
3. **Social Manager** (marketing/content-team) runs read-the-stats: each piece judged against its own stage's metric.
4. Claude compiles `knowledge/MARKETING-CHECKUP-<month>.md`: five sections (competitors · outliers · traffic · content · the one thing to do next), each three lines max, every claim carrying where it came from. The one-thing section is ONE recommendation, not five.
5. Anything that changes what a team is currently building gets flagged to the owner directly, not just filed.
Skills whose prerequisites aren't ready (no key, no stats yet) get skipped WITH a plain line saying so. A checkup that silently skips half its checks is lying.
