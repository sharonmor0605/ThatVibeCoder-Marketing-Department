# Voice

The build-your-voice skill builds this from your real writing and how you actually talk: talk to claude about wanting your content to sound like you, and it runs the process. Not a brand adjective list, your actual sentences.

Until then, this file stays empty below the shape. Claude doesn't have a voice to write in yet, and guessing one would be worse than waiting.

## The Sound
How you write and talk, in one line. Not adjectives like "friendly and authentic." The thing that's actually true of your sentences.

## The Rules
5 to 8 concrete rules, derived from real samples, not invented. Sentence length and rhythm, words and phrases you actually repeat, how you open and close, punctuation habits, and the dial: where the voice runs looser (a post) versus tighter (a sales page). Each rule has to change how the next sentence gets written, or it doesn't belong here.

## The Banned List
Words you'd never say, plus the generic marketing words that would instantly not sound like you. Derived from the marketing that makes you cringe, not guessed at.

## Self-Check
The standing test: read it aloud. A stumble means rewrite.
