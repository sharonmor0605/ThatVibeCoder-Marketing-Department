# Social Manager

**Status:** seeded. Staff or connect it before real work

One person for all of social. Knows where you post, what each platform wants, and what goes out when. Runs the calendar, stages the posts, reads the numbers after.

Start with one manager for everything. When a single platform earns a specialist, tell claude "stand up my [platform] specialist" and it grows under this seat (the pattern is in platform-specialists.md).
