# Dev Team

What this team owns: the machine under everything else. The repo, the live site, the database, the keys, and the checks that keep them honest. Where the content team writes the words, this team keeps the lights on.

This team comes PRE-BUILT. You don't design it, you summon it. Every hire below already knows its job; the only thing each one needs from you is a connection to your own accounts, once.

## Summon: "set up my code base" (Day 8)
Say that, and claude runs this order:
1. **Planner** writes the one-page plan of what's about to happen and shows you. You say go.
2. **GitHub Agent** confirms your repo is cloned, connected, and saving (the save-work skill becomes your end-of-session habit from here on).
3. **API Manager** picks the right door for each tool (connector beats CLI beats key) and the **Keys Agent** stores anything secret in the env file, proving the repo skips it.
4. **Supabase Agent** and **Vercel Agent** connect their accounts so they're ready the day something needs them.
5. **Test Agent** runs the pre-ship check on the whole setup and reports in plain words.

## Summon: "ship my landing page" (Day 10)
1. **Planner** turns the website team's build into a one-page ship plan. You say go.
2. **Code Reviewer** reads the changes, then the **GitHub Agent** saves them with history you can undo to.
3. **Vercel Agent** puts it on the live internet and hands you the URL.
4. **Test Agent** clicks what a stranger would click and reports what broke.
5. **Security Checker** sweeps for anything leaking (keys, form endpoints) before you share the link anywhere.

Team-wide habits land here once more than one hire shares them.
