# ICP

The lock-your-icp skill fills this in: talk to claude about who you sell to, and it runs the interview that replaces this whole file with your answers. Right now it's just the shape.

## Who
The actual person you sell to. Not a category, a real person you could picture.

## Current State
What's actually frustrating them right now, in their own words: the trigger that brought them to you and what they'd already tried before you.

## Dream State
What they actually want to be true instead, ideally in the words they'd use to tell someone about it.

## What Makes Them Click
The proof, the moment, the sentence, whatever actually convinces this person to buy.

## Objections & The Catch
What they were worried about right before saying yes, what settled it, and the catch you tell people upfront. Flat prose, no spin.

## What Does Not Work On Them
The pitch that makes them roll their eyes. What turns this person off completely.

## Who They're Not
The person who looks like a customer but always turns out to be a bad fit, and the early tell.

## In Their Words
5 to 10 verbatim lines pulled from wherever they actually say this stuff: reviews, DMs, a Facebook group, support emails. Future copy gets harvested from these, not written fresh.
