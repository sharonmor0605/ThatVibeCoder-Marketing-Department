## Round 1 — what's been covered so far

**Opening admission (important context, not itself a lens):** The owner only recently realized recruiting is a marketing problem, not just an HR problem — her marketing team had no answer when asked about inbound recruiting, and her recruiter's entire strategy is posting on Indeed. No real inbound strategy exists today.

**Critical stakes, worth remembering:** EDMO does not get paid if seasonal positions go unfilled. This isn't a nice-to-have — recruiting is a direct revenue lever. It also connects to the District Partnerships thread: the same staffing gaps that hurt Chloe's trust also cost EDMO money directly on this side.

**Lens 1 — Who:** **Kimmy.** A recent high school graduate or college student, sometimes someone eyeing graduate school. Generally drawn toward education as a path already, not just looking for any seasonal job.

**Real strategic angle, not just a persona detail:** EDMO sees an opportunity here beyond just filling seats — encouraging these hires toward education as a career, which the owner calls "workforce development." This should shape messaging: the pitch isn't just "we're hiring," it's positioning EDMO as a stepping-stone into an education career.

This is more load-bearing than a typical single-question gap since it's the foundation of lens two — left honestly blank rather than invented.
