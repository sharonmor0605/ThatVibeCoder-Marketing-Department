# write-hooks

The first line is most of the result. It gets its own pass, every time.

## Steps
1. Read the finished draft and name its one real point in one sentence. The hook comes from the point, never from thin air.
2. Write five candidate first lines, different shapes: the specific number, the reader's own frustration in their words, the honest surprise, the thing that happened, the plain claim. All five true.
3. Kill any that overpromise what the piece delivers. A hook the body can't cash burns trust for every post after it.
4. Read the top two aloud back-to-back. The one you'd actually SAY to a person wins. Show the owner both if it's close. The rest die.

## Rules
- The hook is written AFTER the piece, not before. You can't promise what doesn't exist yet.
- Specific beats clever: "3 leads from one page" beats "this changed everything."
- Read it in the owner's voice per brand/voice.md. A great hook in the wrong voice is still wrong.
