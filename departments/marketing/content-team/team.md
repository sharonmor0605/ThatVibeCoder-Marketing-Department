# Content Team

What this team owns: everything you publish. The words, the visuals, the calendar, the numbers after.

Four seats to start, and that's enough: the copywriter writes, the social manager runs where it all goes, the editor is the second pass, the designer makes it look like you. Specialists grow later, one at a time, when a platform or a job earns one.

## Platform specialists
When a single platform earns dedicated attention, tell claude "stand up my [platform] specialist" and it builds the folder under this team from the pattern in platform-specialists.md. Don't pre-create them.

## Memory
Team-wide habits land here once more than one hire shares them.
