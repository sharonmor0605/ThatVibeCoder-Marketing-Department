# find-outliers

## Steps
1. Look at your space's channels and find pieces with engagement at least 10x that account's OWN median (check its last 20 posts to establish the median), posted in the last 60 days. That gap is the signal: the idea beat the audience.
2. For each outlier, write down the shape that made it work, not the topic.
3. File the top three to knowledge/ with one line each on what the content team could do with the shape.
